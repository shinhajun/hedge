How it reads

  1. The audio of the tab you pick (or your microphone) streams to Deepgram, which sends words back about half a second after they’re spoken.
  2. Each new word sends everything said so far to Jev with your asset. It answers with a signal from bearish to bullish and six drivers: guidance, demand, margins, surprise, hedging and legal or policy risk.
  3. When Deepgram corrects a word, every reading after it is redone from the corrected text.
  4. After about 2,000 words, Jev sees the latest 2,000 plus a short note on what came before, so long streams stay fast and cheap.
